# Mathnotes

## Platform

web

## Purpose

Turn naturally spoken or typed mathematical reasoning into clean, editable, typeset documents that sync privately across sessions.

## Users

Students, instructors, tutors, and researchers who think in spoken explanations before formal notation.

## Principles

- Keep the original reasoning readable while typesetting recognized math.
- Make conversion immediate, legible, and reversible.
- Keep each user's documents private with authenticated cloud sync.
- Be honest about what the parser recognizes.

## Capabilities

- Shared dorelboim.com email/password identity through Supabase Auth.
- A personal document library with create, open, search, rename, autosave, and delete.
- Owner-controlled, read-only sharing through unguessable public links.
- Natural-language math conversion rendered with KaTeX.
- Markdown copy and export.
