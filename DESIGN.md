# Design system

Mathnotes uses the visual world of an edited mathematical proof sheet: warm paper, editorial serif text, precise rules, blue notation, and restrained red proof marks. The workspace is the first viewport's thesis, with natural language and typeset output connected by a visible transformation rail.

- Display and prose: Instrument Serif.
- Interface: DM Sans.
- Ground: warm ivory `#f2eee5`; paper `#faf7ef`; ink `#20201d`.
- Mathematical notation: cobalt `#154c92`.
- Editorial marks: oxide red `#b54432`.
- Components use square edges and fine rules, echoing a proof galley rather than generic app cards.
